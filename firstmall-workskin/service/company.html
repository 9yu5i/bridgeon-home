Our Vision

(The Fastest International Shipping from Korea)

TrendyPicker aims to help customers around the world experience Korea's trendy lifestyle in a fast and reliable way.
We go beyond simply selling products—we strive to deliver moments of delight and satisfaction through exceptional experiences.
We believe that the real experience begins the moment an order arrives at your doorstep.
By reducing the inconveniences of international shipping, we hope to bring a healthier and more beautiful lifestyle into the everyday lives of people around the world.

What We Do

(Curated Trendy Korean Products in One Place)

TrendyPicker is an authentic K-Lifestyle platform run by Koreans, delivering a carefully curated selection of popular products from Korea to customers worldwide—fast and reliably.

Our product categories include:
K-Beauty: The latest K-beauty brands and skincare essentials
K-Food: Trendy Korean snacks, ramen, sauces, and more
K-Toys & Goods: Character merchandise, toys, K-pop goods, and collectibles
Office & Stationery: Stylish stationery, planners, and desk accessories
Kitchen & Living: Korean kitchenware and lifestyle goods

All products are available without holding inventory, so you never have to worry about order cancellations due to stock shortages.
Despite being an international shipment, we proudly offer express delivery within 3 days—our standout advantage.

Who We Are

(Global K-Lifestyle platform)

TrendyPicker is a global K-Lifestyle platform curated and operated by real Koreans. We started with the mission to deliver genuine Korean products quickly and accurately to customers worldwide.

Our headquarters are located in South Korea, where we personally manage every step—from order processing to packaging and shipping.

We ship mainly to the U.S., Canada, Europe, and other key markets, with an average delivery time of 2 to 3 days. Plus, enjoy free international shipping on orders from over $59.
