Create an Account

Membership registration is free, and you can start using our website immediately after signing up.

As a member, you will receive mileage points with every purchase, along with various benefits such as member-exclusive discounts and coupons. In addition, you can enjoy a faster and more convenient shopping experience without having to re-enter your shipping address for every order.

Ordering & Payment

You can add multiple items to your cart and place a single order, or choose options and order items individually. We offer various payment methods, including credit card, bank transfer, and direct deposit.

Purchase Benefits

When you make a purchase, you will earn mileage points. Additionally, depending on the purchase amount, you may qualify for extra discount benefits.

Shipping Information

Orders will be shipped via your selected shipping option within 2?? business days. You can track your shipment status anytime through My Page. (Shipping options may vary by country.)

Refunds & Returns

Payment cancellation is possible before the item is shipped. After delivery, refunds are available within 5 days if you cover the round-trip shipping cost. For more details, please refer to our Return & Exchange page.

Additional Information

If you have any other questions, please feel free to contact us through the Q&A board or email us at tp.svc@trendypicker.com. We will be happy to assist you with sincere and attentive support.
